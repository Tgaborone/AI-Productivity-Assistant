# Prompt templates

Email (professional):
"Write a professional email of ~150–200 words with subject '{subject}' to {recipient}. Include these points: {points}. End with a polite call-to-action."

Meeting Summarizer:
"Summarize the following meeting transcript into: (a) 3–5 bullet quick summary; (b) key decisions made; (c) action items with owners and deadlines (if present); (d) one-line gist."

Task Planner:
"Turn this goal into a high-level plan, then break each step into tasks with estimated times and suggested owner roles. Finish with the next 3 immediate actions."

Chatbot safety reminder:
"Always ask clarifying questions if user intent is unclear. Do not produce PII or confidential content. Provide a short disclaimer when asked for legal/medical advice."

Include a short Responsible AI checklist at top of file.
